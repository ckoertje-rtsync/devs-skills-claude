# SES/MB References

The consolidated bibliography behind the `ses-mb-expert` reference files, merged from the four research notes in `../source/notes/`. Each entry gives the full citation, a DOI or URL, and how it was verified: **Crossref** (DOI metadata record), **publisher** (publisher or product page), **title page** (the PDF's cover or title page was read), **catalog** (HathiTrust, Open Library, or a library catalog), **repository** (university repository or DLR elib record), or **OpenAlex only** (index record, no DOI check). "Content read" means the text itself was read, not just the metadata. Anything not checkable is in §6. Author initials appear only where the notes record them. Before reusing an entry in a deliverable, open the source; this list is a verified starting point, not a substitute. Current as of 2026-10-06.
